How accounts work here

The admin
{{ admin_user }}, set with DEALGO_ADMIN_USER and DEALGO_ADMIN_PASSWORD. De-Algo takes that password from the environment on every start, so changing the variable and restarting is the way back in if it is lost — and the reason it cannot be changed on this page.
Everyone else
Uses the feeds: the feed, Focus mode, channels and feeds. Settings, backups and their own sign-ins are their own; Admin, accounts and plugins are the admin's.
Sessions
A signed-in browser holds a random token for {{ session_days }} days. Only a hash of it is stored, so this database is not enough to sign in as anyone. Switching an account off, deleting it, or changing its password ends its sessions at once.
