The model

A Text box on the canvas sends what comes into it to this model, with what you tell it to write, and puts the answer on a pamphlet. It writes when you press Write now on the box, or by itself hourly or daily — never when a page is opened. Using Claude or OpenAI costs money on your account with them, per word read and written.

Claude needs a key from the Anthropic Console; with no model named it uses claude-opus-5-5. For an open-weight model, run a server that speaks the OpenAI chat API — Ollama, vLLM, LM Studio — give its address ending in /v1, and the model's name as that server knows it. The key is kept for this account only, is never shown again, and is not in backups.

Your algorithm

An algorithm of your own, like the one a video site runs on you — but yours, and only on this machine. It learns from how you go through things in Focus mode, and an Aggregation piece on the canvas puts what it predicts to work: under a Filter, under a Sort, or under an Expire box. It learns three things:

  • Interest — would you open it at all, rather than pass it over.
  • Retention — how much of it you would take in: how far into a video, how long on a post.
  • Engagement — how steadily you would stay with it, rather than stopping and starting.
  • Saturation — whether a feed already holds more of a source, or a tag, than you take in. Worked out as it goes, from the feed and what you open: nothing to learn below.
