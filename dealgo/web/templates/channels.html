    Backfill

    How many of the latest posts to run through, from each source this trigger is wired to. They go through the same filters as anything else — this decides how much is offered to them, not what survives.

    A feed lists only its most recent items — usually a dozen or two — so asking for more than it carries reaches as far as it goes and no further.
